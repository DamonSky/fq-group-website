# Welcome to FQ Group

> **Spatio-temporal Chromatinized-omics for Genome Evolution Study**

---

## Research Interests
Development and application of chemical biology and synthetic biology tools in living systems, to provide innovative tools for life science research and develop innovative technologies for diagnostics and therapeutics especially for genetic disease.

---

## Research Directions

### 🔬 1. Decoding Chromatin Dynamics
Development and application of chemical biology, synthetic biology, and multi-omics tools to elucidate the mechanisms underlying dynamic chromatin regulation.

**Key Focus:**
* Chemical Biology
* Synthetic Biology
* Multi-omics

### 💉 2. Gene and Cell Therapy Development
Design, development and application of biological tools for precise disease therapy, especially for hereditary genetic disorders.

**Key Focus:**
* Gene Therapy
* Cell Therapy
* Genetic Disorders

### 🧬 3. Genome Evolution
Focusing on repetitive sequences and transposable elements, we leverage AI-assisted computation combined with stem cell platforms to explore genetic evolution across species.

**Key Focus:**
* AI Computation
* Transposons
* Stem Cells
